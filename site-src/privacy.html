Privacy notice

Last updated 18 November 2026

This notice explains how DPDP Ready ("we") handles personal data, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025. DPDP Ready is an independent, early-stage project based in India. You can reach us at {{EMAIL_HTML}}.

Personal data we collect

  • This website: nothing. It has no forms, cookies, analytics or third-party scripts.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • If you email us: your name, email address, the content of your message, and any company details you choose to share.
  • If you request an assessment: the contact details of the people we work with, plus the scan results and notes needed to write your report.
  • The open-source scanner: it runs on your own machine or CI and sends nothing to us.

Purpose of processing

We use this data only to reply to you, arrange calls, and deliver an assessment you asked for. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or add you to a mailing list.

Consent and how to withdraw it

We process your data because you chose to contact us. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing {{EMAIL_HTML}}. We will then stop processing your data and delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Who else processes it

Email is handled by Google (Gmail), which acts as our service provider. For assessments, we use no other service with your code: any copy is kept only on our own machine and deleted when the assessment ends.

How long we keep it

  • Emails from people who don't become customers: deleted within 12 months of our last exchange, or sooner on request.
  • Assessment working copies of code: deleted when the assessment ends.
  • Assessment reports and invoices: kept for as long as the law requires for business records.

Your rights

You have the right to access a summary of your personal data, to correction and erasure, to grievance redressal, and to nominate another person to exercise your rights. To use any of these rights, email us. We aim to reply within 7 days.

Grievances and complaints

For any concern about how we handle your data, contact our Grievance Officer at {{EMAIL_HTML}}. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

We keep the personal data we hold to a minimum, store it only in accounts protected with strong passwords and two-factor authentication, and never put it in public repositories.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date above. If we start collecting new kinds of data, we will update this notice before doing so.
